Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time to visit Hurghada?▼
The best time to visit Hurghada is from October to April when temperatures are pleasant (20-28°C). However, the Red Sea remains warm and beautiful year-round for diving and snorkeling.
Are hotel transfers included in the tour price?▼
Yes! We provide free round-trip transfers from any hotel in Hurghada. For El Gouna, Makadi Bay, and Sahl Hasheesh, a small surcharge may apply depending on the trip.
How do I book a trip with Aquaway?▼
Booking is incredibly easy! You can book directly through WhatsApp by clicking any "BOOK NOW" button. Our team responds instantly to confirm your spot with no upfront deposit needed for most trips.
Is it safe to go on a desert safari?▼
Absolutely. All our desert safaris are led by professional guides with years of experience. We provide full safety gear (helmets/briefings) and ensure all vehicles are regularly maintained to international standards.
